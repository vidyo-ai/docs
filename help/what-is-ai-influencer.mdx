title: "AI Influencer (Discontinued)"
description: "AI Influencer, the AI avatar video tool in quso.ai, has been discontinued. New avatar videos can no longer be created. What to use instead and existing projects."
---

**AI Influencer** was a quso.ai tool that made talking-head videos with AI avatars from a script. It has been **discontinued**, and you can no longer create new AI Influencer videos. quso.ai now focuses on turning your own videos into clips, captions and posts.

## What happens to my existing AI Influencer videos?

AI Influencer projects you already made still appear in your projects list. Download any videos you want to keep.

## What can I use instead?

- **[AI Clips](/help/what-is-ai-clips):** record yourself once and turn the recording into many short clips.
- **[AI Captions](/help/ai-captions):** add animated captions to a talking-head video you recorded.
- **[AI Writer](/help/how-to-use-ai-writer):** turn a video into scripts, posts and newsletters.
