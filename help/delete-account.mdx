You can permanently delete your quso.ai account yourself, on desktop from **Settings → Account** or on your phone from the **Profile** page. Deleting your account removes all your data, cancels your subscription immediately and cannot be undone.

---

## What happens when you delete your account

- All your data is deleted: projects, clips, downloads and brand assets.
- Your **subscription is cancelled immediately**.
- You are **removed from every workspace** where you are a member.
- Any **workspace you own is deleted**, and its members lose access to it.
- You **can't reactivate** the account within 28 days of deleting it.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account is irreversible. Download any clips you want to keep first.
</Warning>

---

## How to delete your account

### On desktop

1. Go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Click **Delete account**.
3. Type **Delete** in the box, tick the box to confirm you understand your data will be lost, and confirm.

### On your phone

1. Open the **Profile** page.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm in the same way.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I delete my account?">
    If your subscription is on quso.ai's older billing system, the app asks you to contact support first. Email [help@quso.ai](mailto:help@quso.ai) to cancel that subscription, then delete your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm a team member. Does deleting my account delete the team's work?">
    No. A member is only removed from the workspace. The workspace and its subscription belong to the owner. If you are the owner, your workspace is deleted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I just cancel my plan instead?">
    Yes. Cancelling keeps your account on the Free plan. See [Upgrade & downgrade](/help/upgrade-downgrade-plans#cancel-your-plan).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
