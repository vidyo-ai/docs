quso.ai transcribes and captions video in **99 languages** with **AI Captions**. **AI Clips** works in **19 languages**, counting 4 English variants, and **AI Writer** in 20. You pick the spoken language of your video on the import screen, and it must match what is spoken for clips to be found.

---

## AI Clips languages (19)

- English (Global), English (US), English (UK), English (Australia)
- Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch
- Hindi, Chinese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese
- Finnish, Russian, Turkish, Ukrainian

**AI Writer** supports the same languages, plus **Japanese**.

<Warning>
  Choose the language that is actually spoken in the video. If you select English for a French video, no clips are generated, and the credits are refunded.
</Warning>

---

## AI Captions languages (99)

AI Captions supports all of the languages above, plus:

Afrikaans, Albanian, Amharic, Arabic, Armenian, Assamese, Azerbaijani, Bashkir, Basque, Belarusian, Bengali, Bosnian, Breton, Bulgarian, Burmese, Catalan, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Estonian, Faroese, Galician, Georgian, Greek, Gujarati, Haitian Creole, Hausa, Hawaiian, Hebrew, Hungarian, Icelandic, Indonesian, Japanese, Javanese, Kannada, Kazakh, Khmer, Lao, Latin, Latvian, Lingala, Lithuanian, Luxembourgish, Macedonian, Malagasy, Malay, Malayalam, Maltese, Maori, Marathi, Mongolian, Nepali, Norwegian, Norwegian Nynorsk, Occitan, Pashto, Persian, Polish, Punjabi, Romanian, Sanskrit, Serbian, Shona, Sindhi, Sinhala, Slovak, Slovenian, Somali, Sundanese, Swahili, Swedish, Tagalog, Tajik, Tamil, Tatar, Telugu, Tibetan, Turkmen, Urdu, Uzbek, Welsh, Yiddish and Yoruba.

All languages are available on every plan, including Free.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can quso.ai make clips from a Japanese or Arabic video?">
    Not with AI Clips. You can still add captions to those videos with AI Captions, which supports 99 languages, and Japanese works in AI Writer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does quso.ai translate captions or dub videos?">
    No. quso.ai transcribes and captions in the language that is spoken in the video. It does not translate or dub.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my video mixes two languages?">
    Choose the main spoken language. Words in other languages may be transcribed less accurately.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
