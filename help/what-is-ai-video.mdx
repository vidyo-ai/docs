---

**Script to AI Video** (also called the AI Video Generator) was a quso.ai tool that turned a script or idea into a faceless video with stock visuals, a voiceover and subtitles. It has been **discontinued**, and you can no longer create new AI videos. quso.ai now focuses on turning your own videos into clips, captions and posts.

## What happens to my existing AI videos?

AI Video projects you already made still appear in your projects list. Download any videos you want to keep.

## What can I use instead?

- **[AI Clips](/help/what-is-ai-clips):** turn a long video into many short, captioned clips.
- **[AI Captions](/help/ai-captions):** caption any video in 99 languages.
- **[B-roll in the editor](/help/editor/add-broll-to-video):** add stock footage over your own clips.
