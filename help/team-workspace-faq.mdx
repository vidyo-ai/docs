Team workspaces let several people share one quso.ai plan, its credits, projects and brand kits. The owner pays for the plan and for each team member seat. These answers cover the most common questions.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I add more than 4 team members?">
    Yes. A workspace supports 4 members by default. Email [help@quso.ai](mailto:help@quso.ai) to add more.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do team members cost extra?">
    Yes. Each member needs a team member seat, which is a paid add-on billed on the same cycle as the owner's plan. The price is shown before you confirm an invite.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What roles are there?">
    Two: **owner** and **member**. Only the owner can manage billing, buy add-ons and remove members.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do members share credits?">
    Yes. All members use the workspace's credits. Only the owner can buy more.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I be part of multiple teams?">
    Yes. Each team has its own workspace. Switch between them with the workspace menu at the top left of the dashboard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can teammates review posts before they go live?">
    Yes. Use [Send posts for approval](/help/send-post-for-approval).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the workspace if the owner deletes their account?">
    The workspace is deleted and all members lose access. See [Delete your account](/help/delete-account).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
