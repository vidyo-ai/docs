Your quso.ai account is ready to use the moment you sign up. Every free account gets **75 credits a month** (about 75 minutes of video) with no credit card, and your first import can be up to 150 minutes long. To get the most out of quso.ai, work through this short checklist.

### 1️⃣ Set up your brand kit & template

Add your logo, colors and fonts so every clip is on brand.

### 2️⃣ Connect your social accounts

Publish and schedule clips straight from quso.ai.

### 3️⃣ Add team members

Share credits, projects and brand kits with your team.
