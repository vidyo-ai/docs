**TikTok auto-post** posts the rest of a project's clips to your TikTok account for you, one at a time on a schedule you choose. It is a **Free plan** feature: after you publish your first clip from a project to TikTok, quso.ai offers to post the other clips every **3, 6, 12, 24 or 48 hours**, starting with the clips that have the highest predicted views.

---

## Before you start

- You are on the **Free plan**.
- Your TikTok account is connected. See [Connect TikTok account](/help/connect-tiktok-account).
- Your project has clips you haven't posted yet.

---

## Turn on auto-post

1. Publish a clip from your project to TikTok.
2. When the post goes out, you'll see **Keep the momentum going** with the number of clips left to post.
3. Under **Post every**, choose **3h**, **6h**, **12h**, **24h** or **48h**.
4. Click **Turn on auto-post**.

The next clip goes out after the interval you picked, then one clip every interval until all the clips in the project are posted.

---

## Check or stop auto-post

You can see every project that is auto-posting, with the clips left and the time of the next post, in:

- **Settings**, in the social accounts section under **TikTok auto-post** (desktop)
- The **Profile** page (mobile)
- The card that appears after you publish a clip

Click **Stop auto-post** to turn it off. Clips that were already posted stay on TikTok. Disconnecting your TikTok account also stops auto-post.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which clips are posted first?">
    The clips with the highest virality score, so your strongest clips go out first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does auto-post count toward my monthly post limit?">
    Yes. The Free plan allows up to 25 social posts a month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pick an exact date and time for each post instead?">
    Yes, with scheduling on the Essential or Growth plan. See [Publish or schedule posts to multiple platforms](/help/how-to-bulk-publish).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does auto-post work for Instagram or YouTube?">
    No. Auto-post is for TikTok only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
