The quso.ai editor lets you change any clip after AI Clips or AI Captions has made it: captions, layout, aspect ratio, text, logos, music, B-roll, templates and more. Open a clip and click **Edit** to start. The full editor works on desktop; on a phone you can review, download and publish clips. Pick a topic below.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I rotate or flip a video in the editor?">
    No. Rotate the video before you upload it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the editor on my phone?">
    The full editor is on desktop. On a phone you can review, download and publish clips. See [Use quso.ai on mobile](/help/use-quso-ai-on-mobile).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does editing a clip use credits?">
    No. Credits are only used when you import a video.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What resolution do exports have?">
    1080p on paid plans and 720p with a watermark on the Free plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
