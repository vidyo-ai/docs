Adding a background to your clips can help enhance their visual appeal, make text stand out, and align your videos with your brand’s style. In Quso.ai, you can choose from stock backgrounds or upload your own custom images.

## 🎬 How to Add a Background

### **Step 1: Open Your Project**

- From the **All Projects** tab, select the video project you want to personalize.

---

### **Step 2: Open the Clip in the Editor**

- Click on the clip you want to edit.
- Click **Edit** to open it in the editor.

![Edit Captions1 Jp](/images/edit-captions1.jpg)

---

### **Step 3: Go to the Background Option**

- On the left-hand side navigation bar, click the **Background** button.

![Add Background 1 Jp](/images/add-background-1.jpg)

---

### **Step 4: Choose Your Background Style**

You have two options:

1. **Use Stock Backgrounds** — Browse and select from available stock background images.
2. **Upload Your Own Background** — Click **Upload Image** to add a custom background from your computer.

![Add Background 2 Jp](/images/add-background-2.jpg)

---

### **Step 5: Adjust the Background Color**

- If your clip already has a background color, click the color box to choose a new color that fits your style.

![Add Background 3 Jp](/images/add-background-3.jpg)

---

## ✅ You’re All Set\!

Your clip now has a personalized background that aligns with your video’s theme.
