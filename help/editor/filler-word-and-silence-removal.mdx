Quso.ai offers an **auto-removal feature for filler words and silences** to help you create cleaner, more professional videos — effortlessly. This tool eliminates unnecessary pauses or phrases like “uh,” “um,” or long silences, saving you editing time and making your content more engaging.

## 🚀 Steps to Enable Auto Filler Word & Silence Removal

### **Step 1: Upload Your Project**

- Start by uploading your video to [Quso.ai](https://quso.ai).
- ⚠️ **Important:** This feature must be enabled **during the upload process**. If not activated at this stage, it **cannot be turned on later** for that project.

### **Step 2: Enable the Feature in Project Settings**

- While uploading, go to the **Project Settings panel**.
- Toggle on **"Remove Filler Words & Silences"** before finalizing the upload.

![Filler Words 1 Jp](/images/filler-words-1.jpg)

### **Step 3: Access and Toggle in the Editor or Preview**

- Once your project is processed:
  - Open the **Preview Page** or **Editor**.
  - Under the **Magic Tools** section, you’ll find an option to **toggle this feature on/off** to see the effect in real time.

![Filler Words2 Jp](/images/filler-words2.jpg)

## 💡 What This Feature Does

- **Removes filler words** like _um, uh, like, you know_
- **Trims long pauses and silences**
- Helps keep your final video **concise and polished**
