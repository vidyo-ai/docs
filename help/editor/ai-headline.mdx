**AI Headline** opens every clip in your project with a short, attention-grabbing headline. quso.ai writes the headline from each clip's hook and shows it on the video for the **first 5 seconds**, so viewers know right away why they should keep watching.

<Note>
  AI Headline is available for **AI Clips** projects.
</Note>

---

## What the headline looks like

- Bold black text in a white rounded box, centered near the top of the video.
- It sits above the speaker and stays clear of your captions.
- It fits every aspect ratio: on portrait and square clips it spans most of the width; on landscape clips it wraps to about 2 lines. Longer headlines shrink to fit the box.
- On clips shorter than 5 seconds, the headline ends with the clip.
- If your clip uses a template that already has its own title slot, the template keeps its own style and timing and simply shows the AI headline text. A clip never shows two titles.

---

## Turn on AI Headline when you import a video

### Step 1: Start an AI Clips project

- Upload or import your video and choose **AI Clips**.

### Step 2: Switch on the headline

- Under **AI Video Tools**, turn on **Open every clip with an AI headline**.

![AI Video Tools on the import screen, with the AI headline switch on](/images/ai-headline-import.jpg)

<Tip>
  quso.ai remembers your last choice on this switch and uses it for your next import, on any device.
</Tip>

The same switch is available when you import on mobile.

---

## Turn AI Headline on or off after your clips are ready

The headline is **one switch for the whole project**. Turning it on or off applies to every clip in the project.

1. On the page with all your clips, click **AI Tools** at the top.
2. Find **AI headline** and turn the switch on or off.

![The AI Tools menu on the clips page, with the AI headline switch on](/images/ai-headline-ai-tools.jpg)

## Edit the headline on a single clip

The headline is a regular text layer, so you can change it like any other text on your video.

### Step 1: Open the clip in the editor

- Choose the clip and click **Edit**.

### Step 2: Open the text tool

- In the editor's left-hand navigation bar, click the **T** icon. The headline is marked with an **AI headline** badge.

![The headline text layer in the editor, marked with the AI headline badge](/images/ai-headline-editor.jpg)

### Step 3: Reword, retime, or restyle it

- **Reword:** edit the text. Your new wording applies to **this clip only**.
- **Retime:** change the start and end time if you want it to show for longer or later.
- **Restyle:** change the font, color, and effects with the normal text tools. See [Change text style and effects](/help/editor/change-text-style-and-effects).

<Tip>
  Short and specific wins: aim for **4 to 7 words**.
</Tip>

Your reworded headline stays in place if you later switch the clip's template or aspect ratio.

### Remove the headline from a clip

- In the text tool, delete the layer marked **AI headline**.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see a headline on my clips?">
    AI Headline may be off for this project. Click **AI Tools** on the page with all your clips and turn on **AI headline**. AI Headline works on AI Clips projects only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I change the headline on one clip, do the other clips change?">
    No. Each clip has its own headline text. Only the on/off switch applies to the whole project.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does turning it on in one project turn it on in all my projects?">
    No. Each project has its own switch. On the import screen, quso.ai starts from your last choice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
