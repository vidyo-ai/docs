The longest video you can import into quso.ai depends on your credits, because **1 credit covers 1 minute** of video. On the Free plan your **first import can be up to 150 minutes**, and videos linked from sites other than YouTube, Google Drive and Zoom can be up to **4 hours** long. Any aspect ratio works.

---

## Length limits

| Situation | Longest video |
|---|---|
| Any import | The number of credits you have left, in minutes |
| First import on the Free plan | 150 minutes |
| Links from Twitch, Vimeo, Loom, Dropbox, X, Facebook, LinkedIn or Rumble | 4 hours |

To import a longer video, split it into parts or buy more credits. See [How credits work](/help/how-credits-work).

---

## What kind of video works best

- **Spoken words.** AI Clips finds clips from the transcript, so a video with no speech, such as music or gameplay without commentary, will not produce clips.
- **At least a few minutes long.** Very short videos give few or no clips. See [How many clips can I get?](/help/how-many-clips).
- **Any aspect ratio.** Landscape, portrait and square videos all work.
- **The right language.** Pick the spoken language on the import screen. See [Video languages supported](/help/video-languages-supported).
- **Audio-only files** work in AI Writer, but AI Clips and AI Captions need video.

<Warning>
  iPhone HDR videos can look washed out after processing. This comes from the HDR setting on the iPhone. Turn off HDR video in the iPhone camera settings before recording.
</Warning>
