A quso.ai team workspace lets you work on the same projects, brand kits and credits with your teammates. The workspace **owner** invites members by email, and each member needs a **team member seat**, which is a paid add-on to the owner's plan.

---

## Roles

A workspace has two roles:

- **Owner:** the account that created the workspace. Only the owner can buy a plan, seats, credits or other add-ons, change billing and remove members.
- **Member:** everyone else. Members can work on the workspace's projects and use its credits, but can't change billing.

Every invite adds the person as a member.

---

## Invite a team member

1. Click **Invite** on your dashboard. You can also go to **Settings → Workspace & team details**.
2. Enter your teammate's email address.
3. If you don't have a free seat, quso.ai shows the cost of the extra seat before you confirm. Seats are billed on the same cycle as your plan, and the first charge is prorated.
4. Your teammate gets an email and can accept or decline the invite.

<Info>
  A workspace supports up to **4 members** by default. To add more, email [help@quso.ai](mailto:help@quso.ai).
</Info>

---

## What members share

- The workspace's **credits**
- **Projects** made in the workspace
- **Brand kits** and templates in the workspace

---

## Remove a member or leave a workspace

- The owner can remove a member from **Settings → Workspace & team details**.
- A member can leave a workspace from the same page.
