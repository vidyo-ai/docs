**Social Media AI Analytics** in quso.ai shows how your connected social accounts are performing in one place: followers, impressions, engagement rate, posts published, audience demographics and AI-written insights, plus a **Social Score** for each account. It is included on the **Essential** and **Growth** plans.

---

## What you can see

- **Social Score:** a score for each connected account, compared across platforms.
- **AI Insights:** short, AI-written takeaways about what is working and what to try next.
- **Platforms overview:** followers, impressions, average engagement rate and posts published for each network, compared with the previous period.
- **Platform details:** open a network to see performance charts, audience demographics such as top countries, and a table of your posts with their results. For YouTube you also see average view percentage and average time watched.
- **Recent posts:** your latest posts and how they did.

---

## Open analytics

In the sidebar, open **Social Media → AI Analytics**. Choose a Social Set from the dropdown if you have more than one.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why are my LinkedIn analytics empty?">
    LinkedIn only shares analytics for **company pages**, not personal profiles. Connect a company page to see LinkedIn analytics and a Social Score.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does an account show as not linked?">
    The account was disconnected or its access expired. Reconnect it from your social accounts page. See [Social media troubleshooting](/help/social-media-troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which plans include analytics?">
    Essential and Growth.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
