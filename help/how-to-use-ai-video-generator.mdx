---

**Script to AI Video**, quso.ai's text-to-video generator, has been **discontinued**. You can no longer create new AI videos from a script, so the steps that used to be on this page no longer apply.

## Keep your existing videos

AI Video projects you already made still appear in your projects list. Open them and download any videos you want to keep.

## What to use instead

- **[AI Clips](/help/how-to-use-ai-clips):** turn a long video into short, captioned clips.
- **[AI Captions](/help/ai-captions):** add captions to any video.
- **[AI Writer](/help/how-to-use-ai-writer):** turn a video into written content.
