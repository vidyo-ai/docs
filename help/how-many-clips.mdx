---
title: "How many clips can I get?"
description: "How many clips quso.ai's AI Clips makes depends on video length: about 4–12 from a 10-minute video, up to 60 from 2+ hours. Clips run about 20–65 seconds."
---

The number of clips **AI Clips** finds depends on how long your video is and how much of it is speech. Longer videos give more clips. Each AI clip is usually **about 20 to 65 seconds** long, and longer sections appear as **Chapters**.

---

## Typical number of clips by video length

| Video length | Typical number of clips |
|---|---|
| Under 3 minutes | Few or none |
| 3–10 minutes | 4–12 |
| 10–30 minutes | 8–20 |
| 30–60 minutes | 12–30 |
| 60–120 minutes | 18–40 |
| Over 120 minutes | 22–60 |

These are typical ranges, not guarantees. A video with long silent parts, music or several languages gives fewer clips.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did my video produce no clips?">
    The most common reasons are that the video is very short, has little speech, or the wrong language was selected on the import screen. Credits for a project that fails are refunded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does getting more clips cost more credits?">
    No. Credits depend on the length of the imported video, not on the number of clips.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
