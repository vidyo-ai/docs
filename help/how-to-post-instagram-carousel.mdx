---

You can publish an **Instagram carousel** of up to **10 images** directly from quso.ai, without adding the files to your media library first. Carousels use the external content feature, which is on the **Essential** and **Growth** plans. Your Instagram account must be a Business or Creator account. See [Connect Instagram account](/help/connect-instagram-account).

---

### Step 1: Open the Posts page

From the sidebar, go to **Social Media → Posts**.

### Step 2: Create a new post

Click **New Post**.

### Step 3: Upload your carousel images

Select up to **10 images** (JPG, PNG or GIF, up to 20 MB each). To pick several at once, hold **Shift** or **Ctrl/Command** while selecting.

<Note>
  quso.ai keeps the files in the order you upload them, and that is the order they appear in the carousel. Upload them in the order you want.
</Note>

### Step 4: Choose Instagram and add your caption

Select your Instagram account and add your caption (up to 2,200 characters) and hashtags (up to 5 on Instagram).

### Step 5: Publish or schedule

- Click **Publish Now** to post right away, or
- Choose **Schedule** and pick a date and time.
