quso.ai works in your **phone's web browser**: open [app.quso.ai](https://app.quso.ai) on your phone and you get a version built for small screens. There is no iOS or Android app to install. On mobile you can create AI Clips and AI Captions projects, review and download clips, publish to your social accounts and manage your account.

---

## What you can do on mobile

- **Sign in** with Google, Facebook or email. Microsoft sign-in is on desktop only.
- **Make AI Clips:** upload a video or paste a link, choose the language and switch on AI tools such as [AI Headline](/help/editor/ai-headline).
- **Make AI Captions** for a whole video.
- **Review clips:** watch, sort by virality score and download your clips.
- **Publish to social media** from your phone.
- **Manage your account** from the **Profile** page, including stopping [TikTok auto-post](/help/tiktok-auto-post) and [deleting your account](/help/delete-account).

---

## Upload limits on mobile

| Project | Free | Paid plans |
|---|---|---|
| AI Clips | 15 GB | 25 GB |
| AI Captions | 15 GB | 15 GB |

---

## What needs a computer

The advanced video editor, brand kit setup, team management and the content planner are on desktop. Open [app.quso.ai](https://app.quso.ai) on a computer and sign in with the same account. Your projects are there too.
