Post and schedule to a **Telegram channel or group** from quso.ai. quso.ai publishes through **Ayrshare**, so you add Ayrshare's bot to your channel or group as an admin, then send it a one-time code.

<Note>
  Telegram is available on plans that include social publishing, such as **Essential** and **Growth**. You need to be an **admin** of the channel or group.
</Note>

---

## Link Telegram

### Step 1: Copy your link code

1. Go to **Social Media → Posts** and click **Manage accounts**.
2. Click **Link** on **Telegram**. A new window opens with a code that starts with **Code:**.
3. Copy the whole code, including `Code:`.

![Manage social media accounts, with Telegram among the networks you can link](/images/social-networks-manage-accounts.jpg)

### Step 2: Add the bot as an admin

In the Telegram app, open your channel or group and tap its name to open **Info**.

- **Channel:** open **Administrators → Add Admin**.
- **Group:** tap **Edit → Administrators → Add Admin**. If asked, add the bot as a member first.

Search for **@AyrshareBot** (display name "Ayrshare") and select it.

<Warning>
  Pick **@AyrshareBot** exactly. Look-alike bots such as @ayrshare_bot are not ours. The bot must be an **administrator**: if it's only a member it never sees your code and linking fails without an error.
</Warning>

### Step 3: Give it permissions

Turn on all permissions **except the last two**, so the bot can post and delete posts, then tap **Done**.

### Step 4: Send the code

Paste the `Code:` message into the channel or group chat and send it. A success message appears in the chat, usually within a minute and at most 5 minutes. Your channel or group now appears on the Manage accounts page.

---

## What you can post to Telegram

- **Caption:** up to **1,024 characters**. Text-only posts are fine.
- **Media:** **1 image** (up to 5MB) **or 1 video** (up to 20MB) per post. There's no limit on video length, only on file size.
- Posts in a **channel** show your channel's name and logo. Posts in a **group** show as sent by the Ayrshare bot.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I sent the code but nothing happened">
    Make sure the bot is an **administrator**, not just a member, and that it's **@AyrshareBot**. Then send the code again. The success message can take up to 5 minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My video was refused">
    Telegram takes videos up to 20MB. Use a shorter or smaller clip, or remove Telegram from this post.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
