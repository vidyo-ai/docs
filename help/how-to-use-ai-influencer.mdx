**AI Influencer**, quso.ai's AI avatar video tool, has been **discontinued**. You can no longer create new AI Influencer videos, so the steps that used to be on this page no longer apply.

## Keep your existing videos

AI Influencer projects you already made still appear in your projects list. Open them and download any videos you want to keep.

## What to use instead

- **[AI Clips](/help/how-to-use-ai-clips):** turn a recording of yourself into short, captioned clips.
- **[AI Captions](/help/ai-captions):** caption a talking-head video in 99 languages.
- **[AI Writer](/help/how-to-use-ai-writer):** write scripts and posts from your videos.
