Post and schedule to **Threads** from quso.ai. Threads accounts are part of Instagram, so you link Threads by signing in with **Instagram**.

<Note>
  Threads is available on plans that include social publishing, such as **Essential** and **Growth**.
</Note>

---

## Link Threads

### Step 1: Open Manage accounts

Go to **Social Media → Posts** and click **Manage accounts**.

![Manage social media accounts, with Threads among the networks you can link](/images/social-networks-manage-accounts.jpg)

### Step 2: Click Link on Threads

A new window opens.

### Step 3: Sign in with Instagram

Sign in with the Instagram account your Threads profile belongs to. If you're already signed in, you'll go straight to the next step.

### Step 4: Allow access

Grant **all** the permissions Threads asks for, then continue. Your Threads account now appears on the Manage accounts page.

<Tip>
  If you skipped a permission, turn it back on under **Website permissions** in your Threads settings, then link again.
</Tip>

---

## What you can post to Threads

- **Text:** up to **500 characters** per post. Text-only posts are fine.
- **Media:** up to **10 images and videos**, which can be mixed in one post.
- **Video:** up to **5 minutes** each.
- **Long text:** turn on **Post as a thread** to split it into connected posts. See [Post long text as a thread on X and Threads](/help/post-as-a-thread).
