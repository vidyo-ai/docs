You can publish your own images and videos, not just quso.ai clips, straight to your social accounts from quso.ai. Upload the media in a new post, write a caption for each network, then publish now or schedule it. Posting external content is included on the **Essential** and **Growth** plans.

---

## How to publish your own content

### ✅ Step 1: Open the Posts page

From the sidebar, go to **Social Media → Posts**.

### ➕ Step 2: Create a new post

Click **New Post**.

### 📁 Step 3: Upload your media

Select the **images or videos** you want to publish. To pick several files at once, hold **Shift** or **Ctrl/Command** while selecting.

- **Images:** JPG, PNG or GIF, up to **20 MB** each, up to **10** per post
- **Videos:** MP4 or MOV, up to **1 GB**, **1** per post

### 🌐 Step 4: Choose networks and write captions

Select the accounts you want to post to, and add your caption, hashtags and mentions for each one. A post can have up to 30 hashtags (5 on Instagram) and 5 mentions.

<Tip>
  Each network has its own settings, such as YouTube titles, Pinterest boards and TikTok privacy. Check each one before posting.
</Tip>

### 🚀 Step 5: Publish or schedule

- Click **Publish Now** to post right away, or
- Choose **Schedule** and pick a date and time.

## Limits per network

| Network | Caption length | Media per post | Other limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 2,200 | 1 | Up to 20 videos a day; videos up to 10 min and 1 GB |
| Instagram | 2,200 | 10 | Up to 20 posts a day; videos up to 15 min and 1 GB |
| Facebook | 2,200 | 10 | Up to 25 posts a day; Reels up to 90 s |
| YouTube | 5,000 (title 100) | 1 video | Up to 10 posts a day; videos up to 5 GB |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 | 9 | Up to 50 posts a day; videos up to 200 MB |
| X | 280 | 4 | Videos up to 2 min 20 s and 512 MB |
| Pinterest | 500 (title 100) | 3 | Videos up to 15 min and 1 GB |
| Threads | 500 | 10 | Videos up to 5 min and 1 GB; images up to 8 MB |
| Bluesky | 300 | 4 images or 1 video | Videos up to 10 min and 300 MB; images up to 1 MB |
| Telegram | 1,024 | 1 | Videos up to 20 MB; images up to 5 MB |
| Reddit | 5,000 (title 300) | 1 image | No video; images up to 10 MB |
| Snapchat | 500 (Spotlight shows 160) | 1 | Vertical videos of 5 to 60 s |

These limits are set by each network. quso.ai checks them before you post.
