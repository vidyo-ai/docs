Post and schedule to **Bluesky** from quso.ai, alongside your other networks. Bluesky links with your **handle** and an **app password**, not your normal Bluesky password.

<Note>
  Bluesky is available on plans that include social publishing, such as **Essential** and **Growth**.
</Note>

---

## Before you start

Create an app password in Bluesky:

1. In Bluesky, open **Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords**.
2. Click **Add app password**, give it a name (for example "quso.ai"), and create it.
3. Copy the password. It looks like `xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx`.

<Tip>
  An app password only lets quso.ai post for you. You can revoke it in Bluesky at any time without changing your main password.
</Tip>

---

## Link Bluesky

### Step 1: Open Manage accounts

Go to **Social Media → Posts** and click **Manage accounts**.

![Manage social media accounts, with Bluesky, Threads, Telegram, Reddit and Snapchat alongside the other networks](/images/social-networks-manage-accounts.jpg)

### Step 2: Click Link on Bluesky

A new window opens to finish linking.

### Step 3: Enter your handle and app password

- **Handle:** your full Bluesky handle, for example `yourname.bsky.social`.
- **App password:** the one you created above.

Click **Submit**. Bluesky now shows your handle on the Manage accounts page.

---

## What you can post to Bluesky

- **Text:** up to **300 characters**. Text-only posts are fine.
- **Images:** up to **4 images**, each up to **1MB**.
- **Video:** **1 video**, up to **10 minutes** and **300MB**.
- A post has either images or a video, not both.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It says my password is wrong">
    Use an **app password**, not the password you sign in to Bluesky with. Also check that the handle includes the domain, such as `.bsky.social`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my image refused?">
    Bluesky takes images up to 1MB. Compress the image or use a smaller one, or remove Bluesky from this post.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
