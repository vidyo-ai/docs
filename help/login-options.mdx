You can sign up for and sign in to quso.ai with **Google**, **Facebook**, **Microsoft**, or your **email and password**. On a phone's browser, the options are Google, Facebook and email. quso.ai was called Vidyo.ai until January 2025, and Vidyo.ai logins still work.

---

## Which login methods are supported?

| Method | Desktop | Mobile browser |
|---|---|---|
| Google | Yes | Yes |
| Facebook | Yes | Yes |
| Microsoft | Yes | No |
| Email and password | Yes | Yes |

Apple sign-in, magic links and single sign-on (SSO) are not supported.

<Note>
  If you sign in with Facebook, allow quso.ai to see your email address. Without an email, quso.ai cannot create or find your account. Use Google or email instead if you prefer not to share it.
</Note>

---

## Sign up with email

1. Choose **Continue with Email** and enter your email and a password.
2. Open the verification email and click the link. If you can't find it, check your spam folder.

---

## Reset or change your password

- **Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password** on the login page and follow the link in the email.
- **Change your password:** go to **Settings → Account** and use **Change password**. You need your current password. This option only appears for email-and-password accounts.

---

## Can I change the email linked to my account?

Your email can't be changed in the app. If you are on a paid plan, email [help@quso.ai](mailto:help@quso.ai) from your account email with the new address and the team will change it for you.
