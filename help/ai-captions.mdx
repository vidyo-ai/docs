**AI Captions** adds accurate, animated subtitles to your whole video, without cutting it into clips. It transcribes speech in **99 languages**, works on every plan, including Free, and uses 1 credit per minute of video. Use it when you want to caption a finished video, such as a Reel, a YouTube video or an ad.

---

## How to caption a video

### Step 1: Open AI Captions

On the home page, click **AI Captions** ("Subtitles in 99+ languages"). You can also click **New project** and choose **AI Captions**.

### Step 2: Add your video

Upload a file (MP4, MOV or WebM, up to 15 GB on paid plans and 5 GB on Free), pick it from Google Drive or Box, or paste a link. See [Video import source](/help/video-import-source).

### Step 3: Choose the spoken language

Select the language spoken in the video. See the full list in [Video languages supported](/help/video-languages-supported).

### Step 4: Edit and style your captions

When processing is done, open the project in the editor. Your captions are already on the video, and you can:

- [Fix words or timing](/help/editor/change-or-correct-captions)
- [Change the caption style, font and effects](/help/editor/change-caption-style-font-effects)
- [Change the caption animation](/help/editor/change-captions-animation)
- [Add emojis](/help/editor/add-emoji-to-captions) or [sound effects](/help/editor/add-sound-to-caption) to captions

### Step 5: Export

Download the video with the captions burned in. On paid plans you can also download the captions as an **SRT** file. See [Export transcript](/help/editor/how-to-export-transcript).

## AI Captions or AI Clips?

| | AI Captions | AI Clips |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | Your whole video, captioned | Many short captioned clips |
| Languages | 99 | 19 |
| Best for | Finished videos, Reels, ads | Podcasts, webinars, long videos |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is AI Captions free?">
    Yes. It works on the Free plan with your 75 monthly credits. Free exports are 720p with a quso.ai watermark.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I download an SRT file?">
    Yes, on paid plans (Lite, Essential, Growth and Plus).
  </Accordion>
