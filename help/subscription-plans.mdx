quso.ai has a **Free** plan and three main paid plans: **Lite**, **Essential** and **Growth**. Every plan gives you a monthly allowance of credits, and 1 credit covers 1 minute of video you import. Paid plans remove the watermark, export in 1080p and add more storage, publishing and editing features.

<Note>
  The prices below are the standard US prices on [quso.ai/pricing](https://quso.ai/pricing). The price and the plans you see in the app can depend on your country and device. Some accounts see monthly credit plans or one-time credit packs instead. See [How credits work](/help/how-credits-work).
</Note>

## Free

The Free plan costs nothing and needs no credit card.

- **75 credits a month**, about 75 minutes of video. In a few countries, new accounts get a one-time credit grant instead of a monthly renewal.
- Your first import can be up to **150 minutes** long.
- Exports are **720p with a quso.ai watermark**.
- Publish to **TikTok** (up to 25 posts a month), including [TikTok auto-post](/help/tiktok-auto-post).
- Projects are deleted after **7 days**.

## Lite

For solo creators who want clean 1080p exports without social scheduling.

- **100 credits a month**, 1080p exports with no watermark, **10 GB** storage.
- Virality score, CutMagic, AI description and SRT caption download.
- Publishing to TikTok only.

## Essential

Everything in Lite, plus scheduling and AI tools for regular posting.

- **300 credits a month**, **25 GB** storage.
- Publish and schedule to 12 social networks, post your own media, and plan posts in the [content planner](/help/content-planner).
- [Filler word and silence removal](/help/editor/filler-word-and-silence-removal), [Viddy](/help/what-is-viddy), stock media, custom logo and outro.
- [Social media analytics](/help/social-media-analytics).

## Growth

Everything in Essential, plus full branding for teams and agencies.

- **600 credits a month** at $49. Higher Growth tiers give 900, 1,200, 1,500 or 1,800 credits a month.
- **75 GB** storage and up to 100 social posts a month.
- Custom brand fonts, colors, music and unlimited [brand templates](/help/brand-kit-templates).
- Priority support.

## Plus (mobile)

Plus is a plan sold in the mobile browser. It includes 75 credits a month, 1080p exports with no watermark and 10 GB storage. It does not include social scheduling. Once subscribed, you can also use quso.ai on desktop.
